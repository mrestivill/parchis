# 🎲 Welcome to the Board!

Welcome to our digital living room! This isn't just a piece of software; it's a digital board game designed to bring friends and family together over a classic game of Parchis (Ludo). 

Whether you are here to casually roll some dice or to host a competitive tournament night, you are in the right place. 

## 🗺️ How to use this Wiki
We have organized this guide so you can find exactly what you need. Choose your path:

*   **[The Player's Handbook](Player-Handbook.md):** Rules, how to take your turn, UI layout, chat, and reading your statistics!
*   **[The Host's Lounge](Hosts-Lounge.md):** Room customization, server rules, and moderator tools.
*   **[Help Desk & FAQ](FAQ-Troubleshooting.md):** Troubleshooting connection issues and AFK protection.

## 🖥️ Screen Layout (Desktop)
If you are playing on a computer, the game is divided into three main areas:
1.  **Left Panel (Room & Stats):** This shows the Room Code (top left), the list of players connected to your room, whose turn it is, and their current scores. It also features a "Global Leaderboard" tab to see the top players on the server!
2.  **Center Screen (The Board):** The main Parchis board where all the action happens. The dice also appear right in the middle when it is your turn.
3.  **Right Panel (Social):** This is where you see the live Game Activity Log (who rolled what, who got captured) and the Global Chat window to talk with everyone in the room.

## 🚀 Quick Start Checklist

Ready to jump straight in? Follow these simple steps:

- [ ] **Step 1:** Open the game link provided by your host.
- [ ] **Step 2:** Choose to play instantly as a *"Guest"* or log in/register to save your stats.
- [ ] **Step 3:** Enter the 4-letter Room Code to join your friends.
- [ ] **Step 4:** Pick your favorite color.
- [ ] **Step 5:** Wait for the Host to start the game, then roll the dice!
