# 🛟 Help Desk & FAQ (Troubleshooting)

Games are supposed to be fun, not stressful! If something is acting slightly weird, don't worry—it is usually a built-in feature to keep the game flowing smoothly, or easily fixable. 

Here are the answers to the most common questions:

### 🔌 What happens if my internet disconnects? (AFK Protection)
Don't panic! The game has an "Away From Keyboard" (AFK) protection system. 

If your internet drops or you close your browser by accident, the game won't freeze for everyone else. When your timer runs out, the server will gracefully take over for you. It will automatically roll the dice and move your pieces (if there is an obvious safe move) or skip your turn. Just refresh the page and jump right back in!

### ⏭️ Why did the game suddenly skip my turn?
This usually happens for two reasons:
1. You ran completely out of time on your clock.
2. You rolled the dice, but based on the numbers you got, none of your pieces can physically make a legal move (for example, you need a 5 to exit your home, but your pawns are all asleep and you rolled a 2 and a 4). The game realized you were stuck and passed the turn automatically to save time.

### 🤔 How do we restart the game?
*   **During an active game:** Only the person who created the room (The Host) has the power to press the restart button directly and wipe the board clean.
*   **When the game finishes:** Once someone wins and the game says "Finished", any player can initiate a "Restart Vote". If the majority of players (more than 50%) vote "Yes", the board gets wiped clean and a new match begins automatically.

### 📈 Where are my XP, levels, and statistics?
To save your progress, you **must** be logged into a registered account. "Guest" accounts do not save stats! 

If you are logged in, you can click on your profile to see your detailed analytics. You'll find fun data there like how many games you've won, how many pieces you've captured, and even how lucky you are (how many doubles or ones you've rolled!).

> **💡 Got another problem?**
> If you are the server owner, check the server console logs. If you are just a player, let the person who is hosting the game know so they can check the room settings!
