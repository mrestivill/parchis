# 📖 The Player's Handbook

Welcome, player! When you join a room, you'll see the game board in the center, a chat box to talk with your friends, and your dice ready to be rolled.

Let's walk through how to play and what everything means on your screen.

## 🎯 Taking Your Turn

- [ ] **Check the Clock:** Look at the timer bar! You have a limited time to roll the dice and make your move. If you run out of time, you lose your turn!
- [ ] **Roll the Dice:** Click the glowing dice on the screen. The game automatically rolls two 6-sided dice for you.
- [ ] **Pick a Piece:** Click on the pawn you want to move. The board will highlight safe moves for you. 
- [ ] **Auto-Pass:** If you roll the dice but none of your pieces can make a legal move (e.g., they are trapped behind a barrier), the game will automatically pass your turn after a couple of seconds so everyone doesn't have to wait.

## 🏆 The Core Rules of Parchis

The goal is to get all 4 of your pawns from your "Home" (the sleeping nest) all the way around the board and into the center Goal.

### 1. Leaving Home (Waking Up)
Your pieces start asleep. To move a piece onto the start square of the board, **you must use a 5**. 
*   This can be a direct 5 on one die, or the sum of both dice (e.g., rolling a 2 and a 3).
*   *Note: You cannot use part of a 5 to move another piece. The entire 5 must be used to leave home.*

### 2. Rolling Doubles (The Reward and The Risk)
If you roll doubles (e.g., two 4s), you get to move your pieces and then **roll again!** However, there is a catch:
*   > **⚠️ The Penalty:** If you roll doubles **three times in a row**, you lose your turn, and the last piece you moved is sent all the way back to your Home nest!

### 3. Captures and Safe Zones
If your pawn lands exactly on the same square as an opponent's pawn, you capture them! 
*   **The Reward:** The captured pawn is sent back to their home nest, and YOU get a **+20 space bonus move** on one of your other pieces! (And extra time on your clock).
*   **Safe Zones:** Squares marked with special symbols are Safe Zones. If your piece is on a Safe Zone, no one can capture you. Multiple colors can share a safe square peaceably.

### 4. Barriers (Blockades)
If two pieces of the **same color** land on the same square, they form an impenetrable Barrier. 
*   Nobody can pass a barrier—not even your own other pieces!
*   > **⚠️ Breaking a Barrier:** If you have a barrier and you roll doubles, you are **forced** to break your barrier by moving one of those blocking pieces!

### 5. Reaching the Goal
You must land in the center Goal with an **exact roll**. If you are 3 spaces away and roll a 4, you cannot move that piece.
*   **The Reward:** Reaching the goal with a piece gives you a **+10 space bonus move** for another piece!

## 💬 Social Features

### Global Chat
On desktop, the right panel features a chat box. On mobile, look for the chat icon. Anything you type here is visible to everyone in the room. It's great for negotiating truces or friendly banter! 
*   **A note for players:** Spectators can join the chat too!
*   **A note for spectators:** The Room Host has the power to restrict spectator chat. If you can't type or see messages, the Host likely turned off those specific spectator permissions in the Room Settings.

### Emotes
Next to your dice/profile, look for the smiley face button. You can send animated stickers (Emotes) that pop up directly on the game board for everyone to see.

## 📈 Reading Your Statistics
If you are playing on a **Registered Account** (not a Guest), you level up as you play! You can view your stats by clicking on your profile icon:
*   **Win Rate:** Games Played vs. Games Won.
*   **Combat Stats:** Pieces Captured (how many enemies you sent home) vs. Pieces Lost (how many times you were caught).
*   **Dice Luck:** Want to prove the game hates you? Check your "Ones Rolled" and "Doubles Rolled" counters to see exactly how lucky (or unlucky) your dice have been!

## ⚙️ Personal Game Settings
Every player has their own personal settings drawer (the ⚙️ Gear icon near your profile) that affects *only* your screen, regardless of the room host's rules.

### 🎮 Game Controls
*   **Auto-Roll Dice:** If turned on, the game will automatically roll the dice the exact second your turn starts. Great for playing fast while multi-tasking!
*   **Confirm Move:** If turned on, clicking a piece will only *select* it. You must click it a second time to confirm the move. This prevents accidental misclicks that ruin your game.

### 🎨 Appearance & Audio
*   **Interface Mode:** Switch between Light Mode ☀️ and Dark Mode 🌙.
*   **Board Design:** Change the visual theme of the board (e.g., Neon, Minimalist). *Note: The Host can set a default room theme, but your personal setting overrides it for your screen!*
*   **Sound:** Adjust the Master volume and the distinct Sound Effects (SFX) volume.
